Privacy

quietformed.com · last updated 18 November 2026

Quietform builds websites for small businesses. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit this site or ask us for a demo.

The demo request form

The form doesn’t send anything to us or to any server. When you press the button, your browser copies your answers (business name, Instagram, type, location, what you sell and a site you like) to your clipboard and opens our Instagram messages, where you paste and send them yourself. We only see what you choose to send us there.

What we keep

We keep your Instagram conversation with @quietform.ae and the details in it, to build your demo and talk about your website. Ask us in that chat to delete it at any time. Instagram is run by Meta, and its own privacy policy covers the messaging.

No tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising pixels and no cookies. It only stores one note in your browser for the current tab, so the loading screen is quicker if you reload the page. That note disappears when you close the tab.

Services this site loads

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host it receives your IP address and browser details when you visit.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) serves the typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr serve the animation libraries.
  • The portfolio links open our templates on Framer.

Each of these receives your IP address when your browser loads from it, under its own privacy policy.
